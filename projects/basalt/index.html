Basalt

(01)Art Direction & Photography2025
Introduction

Campaign photography for Basalt's winter collection, starring the actor Solitaire. One figure, one landscape of black sand, and a collection shot as choreography instead of catalogue.

Scope
Campaign
Social
Timeline
4 weeks
(02)Overview

Challenge

Casting a famous face is usually a shortcut, and that was the risk. Solitaire's presence could carry the campaign so easily that the clothes would become costume in someone else's story. Basalt wanted the opposite hierarchy: the collection first, the landscape second, the fame a distant third.

Solution

We built the campaign on movement rather than portraiture. Working with a movement director, we developed a sequence of held, sculptural poses that turn the body into a form the garments drape and break across. The face appears, but rarely straight on, and the ash dusting and hard side light push it toward sculpture.

Process

Weeks one and two were preparation: location scout and permits, garment tests against the sand in matching light, and two rehearsal days with the movement director and a stand-in, building a shot list of 18 held poses timed to the minute.

The ash treatment was tested on set stand-ins until it read as mineral rather than makeup.

(03)Final Thoughts

Conclusion

The collection shipped with a single image system across lookbook, store windows and social, and the silhouette-first frames became the season's most-saved posts. You recognize the clothes first and the actor second, which was the whole brief.
